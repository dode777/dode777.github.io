PressFilter

PressFilter (working title)

See who wrote it — and hide the outlets you would rather skip

A Chrome extension that labels the news outlet on every article in Korean portal news feeds and folds away articles from outlets you choose.

Coming soonGitHub repository

The Chrome Web Store listing is being prepared.

Overview

In portal news listings the publishing outlet is often printed small or left out entirely. PressFilter attaches a clear outlet label to each article and tidies the list by folding away articles from outlets you have chosen to skip.

The aim is tidying rather than blocking: folded articles are meant to stay one click away if you want to read them anyway.

The extension is still in development and is being prepared for the Chrome Web Store. Timing and feature set may change as work continues.

Features

  • Outlet labelsPlanned

    Shows the publishing outlet clearly on each article in news feeds and search results.

  • Hide by outletPlanned

    Nominate outlets you would rather not see and their articles are folded away in the list.

  • Manage your filter listPlanned

    Add and remove outlets from your hidden list at any time.

  • Per-site togglePlanned

    Turn the extension on or off separately for each supported portal.

Install and usage guide

  1. Chrome Web Store listing in preparation

    There is no installable build yet. Once the listing is live, you will be able to install it straight from the button on this page.

  2. One-click install at launch

    After publication, “Add to Chrome” is all it takes. It is being built to start working on portal news pages with no further setup.

  3. Choose outlets to hide

    Open the outlet list from the extension icon and pick the ones to fold away; the setting applies from your next visit.

Frequently asked questions

When can I use it?

It is in development and being prepared for the Chrome Web Store. This page will be updated first once a release date is set.

Does it delete articles?

No. The plan is to fold them out of the way rather than remove them, so you can still open one if you want to.

Will other sites be supported?

Naver and Daum come first. Broader site support is under consideration.

Could the name change?

PressFilter is a working title and may change during the Chrome Web Store submission.

Does it collect my browsing history?

It is being designed not to. A finalised privacy policy will be published alongside the Web Store listing.
