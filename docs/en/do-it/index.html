Do-It

Your day, one gesture at a time

Open in browser

doit.isocompany.co.kr

Open it on a phone or tablet. On a computer you will see a QR code.

Overview

Daily pills, easy-to-forget routines, monthly bills. There is a lot to keep track of, and checklists grow long fast.

Do-It shows one thing per screen. Press and hold to finish it, then swipe up to the next. Missed items are never piled up in red.

Key features

  • Press and hold to finish

    No buttons to hunt for: hold anywhere on the card. Hold again to undo; count goals go up by one each time.

  • Add in one line

    Double-tap and type a line — the time, count and amount are filled in for you.

  • Swipe by topic

    Split things into topics such as pills, routines and chores and swipe sideways between them. Within a topic, swipe up card by card.

  • Intervals and deadlines

    Things you do every few days, or by a set date, show up when they are due.

  • Bills at a glance

    Subscriptions, insurance and utilities grouped by category, with the monthly total and upcoming payment dates.

  • An app without installing

    Opens straight in the browser. Add it to the home screen to open it full screen, and offline after the first visit.

Do-It in use

  • One thing per screen — only what needs doing now, in large type.

  • Press and hold to finish — hold again to undo.

  • Double-tap to add in one line — it splits time, dose and amount for you.

  • Pull down for today’s summary — what is left per topic and your monthly bills.

Add to the home screen

  1. Open it on your phone

    Tap "Open in browser" above, or go to doit.isocompany.co.kr. If it opened inside a messenger app, switch to the default browser first.

  2. iPhone (Safari)

    Tap the Share button at the bottom and choose [Add to Home Screen].

  3. Android (Chrome · Samsung Internet)

    Open the menu (⋮) at the top right and choose [Add to Home screen] or [Install app].

Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

Is it on the App Store?

For now it is a web app only. Open it in the browser and add it to the home screen to get an icon that opens without the address bar.

Can I use it on a computer?

Do-It is driven by gestures — hold, double-tap, swipe — so it supports phones and tablets only. On a computer it shows a QR code to open it on your phone.

I cannot find "Add to Home Screen".

Browsers inside apps such as KakaoTalk or Instagram do not offer it. Reopen the page in Safari on iPhone, or Chrome / Samsung Internet on Android.
